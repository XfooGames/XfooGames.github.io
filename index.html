Privacy Policy for Prospecting Simulator, Dirtbike Engine, and Graffiti Simulator

Last updated: 04.07.2025

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile games "Prospecting Simulator", "Dirtbike Engine", and "Graffiti Simulator", all developed and published by Foxelix (previously XfooGames).

Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personal data from users of these games. "Prospecting Simulator", "Dirtbike Engine", and "Graffiti Simulator" do not require any personal information to function, and we do not collect, store, or share any user data.

Permissions

Each game may request certain permissions to support features and functionality. Below are the permissions required by each app:

Prospecting Simulator

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required for accessing online features and updates.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used to send game notifications.

Dirtbike Engine

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required for downloading assets and updates.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used to send notifications for updates, events, or tips.

Graffiti Simulator

  • No special permissions required: This game is a single-player experience where you can spray graffiti on walls. It does not require internet access, multiplayer features, or collect any personal data.

Local Storage

All games may use the device's local storage to save and retrieve essential game data. This information is stored only on your device and is not shared with Foxelix or any third parties. This data may include:

  • Game progress
  • User settings and preferences

Children’s Privacy

Our games are intended for a general audience, including children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Since our games do not collect personal data, there is no need for parental consent.

If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will promptly delete such information from our systems. Local game data stored on the device, such as settings and progress, is inaccessible to us and cannot be shared.

Upcoming Features

We may introduce new features, such as multiplayer gameplay, in future updates of "Prospecting Simulator", "Dirtbike Engine", or "Graffiti Simulator". These may require additional permissions to function. When any changes are made, we will update this Privacy Policy accordingly.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are encouraged to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any updates by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. Changes are effective immediately after being posted.
